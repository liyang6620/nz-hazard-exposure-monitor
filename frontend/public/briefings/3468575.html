Situation

This assessment uses the official shaking layer. It indicates damaging shaking in populated exposure cells. It applies current building stock and census population. The results describe exposure and do not estimate damage or loss.

Limitations

  • No policy data is used; buildings and population are exposure proxies only.
  • Shaking layers describe modelled or observed intensity, not loss or damage.
  • Building appearance and disappearance dates are approximate where source history is incomplete.

Recommended checks

  • Review the mapped shaking footprint before operational use.
  • Check for updates to the authoritative shaking source.
  • Verify the exposure totals against the published event summary.
