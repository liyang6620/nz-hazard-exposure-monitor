# ADR 0006: M5 and M8 presentation deliverables

Status: accepted by the repository owner, 6 November 2026 NZDT.

## Context

The M5 Power BI report was rebuilt as a five-page PBIP and reviewed from
full-canvas screenshots and a Desktop-exported PDF. A two-minute recording was
also prepared, but the owner cancelled the video requirement during PR #59
review. The M8 interview material is a five-minute written script.

## Decision

M5 deliverables are the source-controlled PBIP, five full-canvas screenshots,
and one five-page PDF. No M5 or M8 video is required. Keep
`docs/interview-demo.md` as the five-minute interview demonstration script.

## Consequences

Reviewers inspect the PBIP and static evidence directly. The report remains
local-only: no Microsoft sign-in or Power BI Service publication is needed.
